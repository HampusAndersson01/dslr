## Design Context

### Users
The app is for a photographer controlling a Nikon D5200 from an Android phone in the field. The core job is fast camera connection, live-view control, capture retrieval, and immediate private photo critique.

### Brand Personality
Practical, focused, and camera-native. The interface should feel like a field tool: direct, calm, and reliable rather than decorative.

### Aesthetic Direction
Use a warm light theme with dark preview surfaces, large touch targets, and task-based navigation. Avoid generic dashboard clutter, gimmicky AI styling, and burying camera controls below long text.

### Design Principles
Keep camera connection, shooting, and coaching in separate task views.
Make current camera state and retrieval state visible.
Prioritize one-handed touch use with large controls.
Render AI output as readable critique, not raw model text.
Keep recovery actions close to failure messages.
